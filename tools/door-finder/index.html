There is no support phone number. Facebook, Instagram and Google do not offer a customer-service phone number for ordinary users. Any “support number” in an ad, a comment, or a search result is a scam.

Before it happens again

Turn on two-factor authentication — the setting that asks for a code from your phone as well as your password when you sign in. It takes a few minutes. In 2026, when one of Meta’s own rescue tools was turned against its users and thousands of Instagram accounts were taken over, the accounts with two-factor authentication turned on held.

Do it on your email account first: email is the master key to everything else, because every “forgot password” reset lands there.
