# Starter Instructions Template — for your AI agent

From the SI Agents series: **Day 1 — The Instructions** (ztrader88 on YouTube).
Intended home once published: ZTrader88-Toolkit repo, `prompts/starter-instructions-template.md`.

---

## What this is

This is the starter version of the one page your agent follows every time it
works for you — the page Day 1 of the series teaches you to write. It is blank
on purpose. The shape is the lesson; the words will be yours.

Words said out loud evaporate. Tomorrow you would say it slightly differently,
and the agent — which can only go by what it is given — would drift with every
retelling. Written down, the instructions hold. That is the whole idea.

## How to use it

1. **Copy the block** between the two lines marked COPY FROM HERE / COPY TO
   HERE, further down this page.
2. **Paste it where your agent keeps its instructions** — the setup or
   instructions page your agent gives you when you create it. (In Day 3's
   piece, the notebook lives beside this page; this page comes first.)
3. **Fill in every part in [square brackets].** The brackets are signposts —
   replace them with your own words and delete the signpost text as you go.
4. **Write the finish line early.** Part 3 — what done looks like — is the
   part people skip and the part that matters most. If you get stuck, write
   that part first and write the rest backwards from it.
5. **Read the finished page out loud once before you save it**, the way you
   would read a note left on the fridge for someone you trust. If a sentence
   sounds foggy in the air, it is foggy on the page.
6. **Keep it short enough to read in a minute.** A page nobody rereads is a
   page that quietly stops being true. When something changes, change one
   thing at a time, so you always know which change did what.

## What each part does

- **Who it works for** — names you and your household, and how you like
  things. It also settles the household question before it becomes an
  argument: if two people instruct the agent differently, whose word wins?
  (The owner's — said kindly and plainly.) And what the others can see of
  what it does: whatever this page allows, and nothing it doesn't. This page
  is the whole and final rule — no later setting quietly overrules it.
- **The job, in plain steps** — the work itself, in the order it should
  happen, in words a person new to your house would understand.
- **What done looks like** — the finish line: one sentence you could check
  yourself, at a glance. If you cannot describe what finished looks like,
  the agent cannot aim at it, and you cannot judge it.
- **The never list** — the shortest, most important paragraph on the page:
  the things the agent must never do without asking you first. The four in
  the block below — sending, spending, deleting, publishing — are the
  series' standard four, because none of them can be taken back once done.
  Keep them, and add your own lines under them. This list is not about
  distrust. It is the same courtesy you would extend to any capable person
  new to your house: here is the work, and here are the doors you do not
  open on your own.
- **A finished example** — one job, done once, done right, done your way,
  pasted or written beside the instructions. An instruction describes the
  standard; an example shows it — and showing lands in a way describing
  never quite does. (In the video, the example is a morning list written in
  the owner's order: family first, then the house, then everything else.
  Yours will be your own job, done your way.) When the agent gets something
  wrong — it will, and so would anyone — the fix goes here, not in a
  scolding: fix the instruction, add the example it was missing, and the
  mistake never has to be paid for twice.

# MY AGENT'S INSTRUCTIONS

## Who it works for

[Your name — and your household. How you like things, in plain words:
the order you like things in, names spelled the way your family spells
them, anything it should always know about who it is working for.]

If two of us tell it different things, [the owner's name]'s word wins.
Others can see: [whatever this page allows — write it plainly].

This page is the whole and final rule. No later setting quietly
overrules this page.

## The job, in plain steps

1. [First step, in plain words.]
2. [Next step.]
3. [Next step. Add or remove lines until the job is fully described.]

## What done looks like

[One sentence: the finish line. Write it so you could check it yourself,
at a glance.]

## Never without asking me first

- Sending anything in my name
- Spending money
- Deleting anything
- Publishing or posting anything
- [Add your own lines here — anything that cannot be taken back once
  it is done.]

## A finished example

[Paste or write the job done once, done right, done your way. This is
the standard the agent aims at.]
